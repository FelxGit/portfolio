Origin

FAITH ASC began as ChronoKnowledge, a personal knowledge base I built during my years at my previous company to keep my own notes organized. The company noticed it and adopted it as a resource for new hires, which pushed me to keep improving it.

It later grew into Community Dev, a general community platform. The video below was recorded during that phase.

Community Dev — before the Faith ASC rebrand

Timeline

  1. ChronoKnowledge Personal notes and knowledge app, later adopted by my previous company as a resource for new hires.
  2. Community Dev Rebuilt as a community platform with posts, profiles, events, points, and an admin panel.
  3. faith-asc.com — AWS phase Deployed on an AWS VPS during the free trial. Added working notification broadcasting and a supervisor that keeps the socket commands alive, upgraded Laravel Mix to Vite, and migrated Vue 2 to Vue 3 — all done manually, without LLM assistance.
